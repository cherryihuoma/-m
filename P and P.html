Privacy Policy

June 2026

We built Îæm on intention. That extends to how we handle your data. We collect only what we need, we use it only for what we say, and we don't sell it. Ever.

01

Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to Îæm, a brand operating under Cherychoicy Classic Communication, based in Nigeria. When we say "Îæm", "we", "us", or "our", we mean this entity.

This policy covers data collected through our website and through our WhatsApp ordering process. It is prepared in accordance with Nigeria's Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Our data controller contact: iaemhq@gmail.com

02

What Data We Collect

We collect information only when you actively give it to us. Here's exactly what and when:

Via our contact form (Formspree):
Name, email address, subject, and message content. This is used solely to respond to your inquiry.
Via WhatsApp ordering:
Name, phone number, body measurements, delivery address, and order preferences. This is used to fulfil your order and communicate with you about it.
Via website browsing:
We do not run analytics or tracking scripts. We do not use cookies beyond what your browser sets automatically. We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party tracking tools.

03

How We Use Your Data

We use your information only for the purpose you provided it. Specifically:

  • To process and fulfil your clothing order
  • To communicate with you about your order status
  • To respond to your inquiries and messages
  • To send you a measurement guide if you place an order
  • To arrange delivery of your completed order

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sell it to any third parties. We do not send unsolicited marketing messages.

04

Who We Share Data With

We share your data only where absolutely necessary to deliver our service:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions on our behalf. Their privacy policy governs data on their platform
  • WhatsApp / Meta — our ordering conversations take place on WhatsApp. Meta's privacy policy applies to data processed on their platform
  • Delivery partners — your name and delivery address are shared with the courier fulfilling your order

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties.

05

How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your order and communication data for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil your order and handle any post-delivery queries — typically no longer than 2 years after your last interaction with us.

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at iaemhq@gmail.com. We will action deletion requests within 30 days.

06

Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access
Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
Correction
Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
Deletion
Request that we delete your personal data
Objection
Object to how we process your data in certain circumstances
Portability
Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
Withdraw Consent
Withdraw consent at any time where processing is consent-based

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at iaemhq@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

07

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data. Communications via WhatsApp are end-to-end encrypted by default. Our website does not store payment information — all transactions are handled through agreed methods in private WhatsApp conversations.

While we work to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but we commit to handling your data with care and discretion.

08

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website or services after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Questions? Concerns? Reach us at iaemhq@gmail.com. We handle data the same way we handle our brand — with intention and without shortcuts.
